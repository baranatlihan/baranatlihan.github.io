Privacy Policy – Color Pop Jam

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Color Pop Jam (Android package: com.baranatlihan.colorpopjam) is developed and published by Baran Atlıhan, an independent developer responsible for the game's data processing.

This policy explains how information is processed to provide gameplay, authenticate guest accounts, verify purchases, prevent duplicate rewards and fraud, handle refunds, deliver advertising when enabled, and respect privacy choices. Data processing depends on the installed game version, enabled features, location, and player choices. Development and testing builds may use different configurations from a publicly released version.

For the intended initial Android Production release, Unity LevelPlay is planned to mediate rewarded and interstitial advertising from ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob. Google UMP supports applicable advertising consent choices. GameAnalytics gameplay analytics remains optional and is intended to collect data only after a separate affirmative player choice. Firebase purchase services and Unity IAP support their respective enabled functions. Bugsnag, AppsFlyer, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics are not activated as optional data-collection services in that release. The actual services used depend on the installed build and enabled features. Development testing or Production preparation does not establish that live ads or Production analytics are already active.

Information We May Process

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language or locale, technical or installation identifiers, IP address, and network or error information needed by enabled services. Providers may derive an approximate country or region from network information.
  • Guest identifiers: a Firebase Authentication anonymous user ID and authentication information used to identify a guest when accessing authenticated purchase services. An anonymous ID is a technical identifier, not a public profile or a guarantee of complete anonymity.
  • Purchase information: products, prices and transaction or order references, purchase history, store receipts or purchase tokens processed for verification, verification and fulfillment results, duplicate-prevention records, refund or revocation status, and related guest or installation identifiers. Unity IAP may process purchase-event information separately from our Firebase verification service.
  • Local game data: progress, coin balances (including negative adjustments following verified refunds), power-up and fuel inventory, settings, and purchase fulfillment or entitlement state stored on your device. The server purchase ledger is not a complete cloud backup of the game.
  • Advertising information, when ads are enabled: advertising identifiers where permitted, device and network information, IP-derived approximate location, ad requests and bidding or mediation events, ad placements, impressions, interactions, rewarded outcomes, impression-level revenue information, fraud-prevention signals, and consent preferences. Advertising networks and demand partners may process these categories according to their roles and policies.
  • Optional gameplay analytics, only when enabled with separate consent: level starts, completions, failures, retries and revives; onboarding, tutorial and gameplay progression; in-game currency and power-up activity; advertisement lifecycle and rewarded outcomes; and limited purchase-related event categories. GameAnalytics may also process pseudonymous SDK, session or installation identifiers and technical device, app or network information.
  • Service and security information: authentication and backend request records, relevant technical errors, transaction validation data, and operational information used for reliability, abuse prevention, and support. This is separate from optional gameplay analytics.
  • Support information: your email address and details you voluntarily provide when contacting the developer.

We do not directly collect payment card numbers or payment card security codes. Google Play and its payment providers handle payment details. Custom gameplay analytics events are not intended to contain raw purchase receipts, full purchase tokens, payment card details, authentication secrets, or personal contact information.

Anonymous Guest Account and Recovery Limits

The game uses Firebase Authentication in Anonymous Guest mode for features such as authenticated purchase verification. Creating this identity does not require an email address or password. The initial Android release does not offer Google sign-in, email sign-in, or account linking.

If you uninstall the game, clear app data, or change devices, restoration of progress, purchased coin balances, and other locally stored information is not guaranteed. You may lose the local records or receive a new anonymous account. Google Play purchase history is not a backup of previously spent or remaining coins or gameplay progress.

Remove Ads is a permanent, non-consumable store product, but automatic restoration to a new anonymous account or device is not guaranteed. Restoration depends on available store evidence, account binding, and verification. Previously consumed coin purchases are not granted again merely because they appear in historical store records.

These technical limitations do not limit applicable consumer, refund, or data protection rights. Contact us for purchase issues; recovery depends on the available records and cannot be guaranteed in every case.

Google Play Purchases, Verification, and Refunds

When in-app purchases are available, Google Play processes payments for Small Coin Pack (250 coins), Medium Coin Pack (750 coins), Large Coin Pack (2,000 coins), and Remove Ads. The game does not directly handle payment card details.

Color Pop Jam uses Firebase Cloud Functions and Google Play purchase APIs to verify purchases before granting in-game coins or entitlements. Cloud Firestore stores purchase authorization and reconciliation records to track fulfillment, prevent duplicate grants and fraudulent claims, and process verified refunds or revocations. Relevant records are associated with pseudonymous guest, installation, transaction and hashed identifiers. The Firestore purchase ledger is not intended to retain full raw purchase receipts or tokens.

The intended Android Production configuration keeps Production financial records separate from Development and testing financial records. Existing Development purchases, coins, and Remove Ads test entitlements are not automatically carried into a new Production financial wallet. Older transaction and security records may be retained for legitimate verification, reconciliation, and fraud-prevention purposes; they are not automatically deleted or migrated when a Production version is introduced.

Verified refunds or voided purchases can reverse their associated in-game benefits. For a refunded consumable coin purchase, the corresponding coins may be deducted. If some of those coins have already been spent, the in-game coin balance may become negative. Future in-game coin earnings or purchases may offset that negative balance. Ordinary coin spending is not intended to create additional negative balances. A negative coin balance is an in-game adjustment, not an external monetary debt or a charge against a payment method.

A verified revocation of Remove Ads may remove the related advertising entitlement, subject to any other valid entitlement. Transaction and refund tracking is designed to prevent the same grant or revocation from being applied repeatedly. Purchase verification and refund processing are essential commerce and security functions and do not require optional gameplay analytics consent.

Unity In-App Purchasing Data Processing

When purchasing is enabled, Color Pop Jam uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP), version 5.4 or later. Unity IAP can process information for store purchases, transaction reporting, duplicate detection, reliability, and security, separately from our Firebase verification service and optional GameAnalytics.

Depending on the transaction and SDK configuration, this may include product, price, purchase and transaction data returned by Google Play; receipt or transaction payload information; device, application, installation or session identifiers; OS version, device model, language or locale; approximate country inferred from IP address; and associated service data. Unity's necessary purchase processing can occur regardless of optional gameplay analytics consent. This does not mean Unity receives the game's users' payment card numbers.

For the Color Pop Jam projects in Unity Dashboard, we selected essential Developer Data collection and core service usage, disabled additional recommended collection and optional broader usage, and did not grant additional Developer Data access to Unity Ads through those Developer Data settings. These choices do not eliminate processing essential to Unity IAP or processing by Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and ironSource Ads when advertising is enabled. Depending on the activity, Unity may act as a processor/service provider or an independent controller under its applicable terms.

Read Unity IAP's privacy explanation, Unity IAP's Google Play data safety information, Unity's privacy policy, and Unity IAP Additional Terms.

Advertising and Consent

The intended initial Android Production release uses Unity LevelPlay as the mediation platform for rewarded and interstitial ads, with three configured bidding networks: ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob. The game previously used AdMob Direct for Development testing; a LevelPlay-enabled release is intended to use LevelPlay rather than initialize a separate AdMob Direct advertising provider alongside it. The final live configuration remains subject to release and device testing.

When advertising is enabled, LevelPlay and participating ad networks may process device and app information, IP addresses and approximate location, advertising or installation identifiers where permitted, consent signals, ad requests, bidding and impression data, ad interactions and rewarded outcomes, and relevant measurement or anti-fraud information. Advertising demand partners or authorized resellers may participate through those networks even when they are not separate LevelPlay mediation adapters.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides applicable advertising consent messages and privacy options. The advertising integration is designed to use relevant UMP, IAB TCF, and Google Additional Consent signals with supported mediation partners where applicable. Depending on region, applicable law, and player choices, advertising may be personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted; some ad requests or SDK initialization may be blocked until the necessary permission is available. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or all essential technical processing lawfully permitted for their delivery.

Where available or required, use the game's Privacy Options control to review or change advertising choices. Android device settings may also provide advertising ID and privacy controls. The Remove Ads purchase is intended to suppress automatic interstitial advertising under the game's entitlement rules; optional rewarded ads may remain available. Buying Remove Ads is not a data deletion request or a substitute for privacy consent.

UMP advertising consent does not authorize GameAnalytics gameplay analytics. These are separate choices. Advertising-related events or impression revenue may be used for optional GameAnalytics reporting only where that separate analytics choice permits it. An optional rewarded ad may grant an in-game benefit after its successful completion and verification.

For further details, see Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (including Unity Ads, LevelPlay, and ironSource), Google's Privacy Policy, and the advertising partner details available in applicable UMP consent messages.

Optional Gameplay Analytics – GameAnalytics

The intended initial Android Production release is designed to offer GameAnalytics gameplay analytics only after the player separately and affirmatively accepts analytics collection. Analytics is not necessary for essential gameplay, Firebase purchase verification, or Google Play purchases. Development or test versions may have Production GameAnalytics collection entirely disabled.

When accepted and enabled, GameAnalytics may process level starts, completions, failures, difficulty and attempt counts; retries and revives; onboarding progression; coin and power-up acquisition, spending and usage; advertisement request, impression and reward events; limited verified purchase-related categories or revenue metadata; session activity; and SDK-generated pseudonymous player or installation identifiers, device/app attributes and network information. This information helps us improve progression, onboarding, game balance, engagement, and economy design.

Development test purchases are not intended to be treated as real Production revenue. Custom analytics events are designed not to send raw store receipts, complete purchase tokens, authentication secrets, payment card details, or personal contact information. GameAnalytics may nevertheless process its own technical identifiers and network metadata as needed for its service.

If analytics consent is declined, skipped, or not yet given, optional GameAnalytics collection is intended to remain disabled. The game's available analytics or privacy settings can be used to change the choice. Withdrawing consent is designed to prevent subsequent optional analytics event dispatch and sessions governed by that consent; it does not necessarily cancel requests already in flight or erase information previously transmitted or temporarily cached by the native SDK.

GameAnalytics may retain data in accordance with its applicable service terms and data processing arrangements. We have not set up a game-data sharing connection with another development organization for Color Pop Jam; this does not exclude authorized GameAnalytics infrastructure or subprocessors. You may contact us about applicable access or deletion requests. Read GameAnalytics privacy information and its privacy notice.

Crash Diagnostics and Other Optional Services

Bugsnag crash reporting is not activated in the intended initial Android Production release. AppsFlyer attribution, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics are likewise not active optional data-collection services in this release. The presence of an unused SDK dependency or development integration does not mean that optional collection is enabled.

Android, Google Play, Unity IAP, Firebase, Google Cloud, and enabled ad services may process diagnostic, security, or operational information needed for their own functions. This is separate from optional Bugsnag crash reporting and GameAnalytics gameplay analytics. If new optional monitoring or attribution services are enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated as necessary.

Firebase and Backend Services

Depending on enabled features, Color Pop Jam uses Firebase Authentication for anonymous guest identity, Firebase Cloud Functions and Google Play APIs for verified purchasing, Cloud Firestore for authorization/fulfillment/refund records, and Firebase Remote Config for supported remote game configuration. Google Cloud infrastructure may also process access, security, service-health, and technical request logs.

These services may process authenticated request details, guest identifiers, installation references, IP addresses, and technical data needed for their respective operations. Firebase Authentication is not an email login or a complete gameplay backup. Necessary backend processing is separate from optional GameAnalytics consent.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress, wallet and inventory data, and settings are generally kept locally until changed or removed through the game or device. Uninstalling or clearing app data can delete local records, but does not automatically delete server-side purchase ledgers, store records, or information already processed by service providers.

Anonymous guest and transaction records are retained as needed to verify purchases and entitlements, prevent duplicate grants or fraud, process refunds and disputes, provide support, and meet applicable legal requirements. There is currently no universal automatic expiration or one-click deletion process for purchase ledger records. We do not promise immediate deletion of every commerce or security record following an uninstall or deletion request.

When GameAnalytics is enabled with consent, the provider may retain event data, technical identifiers, and data previously queued or received under its applicable retention settings. Withdrawing consent prevents future collection as described above but does not itself guarantee immediate removal of data already received by the provider. Google AdMob, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, Google Play, Unity IAP, and other providers may also maintain their own records in accordance with their policies.

For access or deletion requests associated with your guest identity, contact baranatlihan9@gmail.com and identify the game as Color Pop Jam. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify the request and locate the relevant anonymous account or purchase records. If access to the original anonymous identity has been lost, record identification may be difficult. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, full purchase tokens, or payment card details.

Requests are reviewed under applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for lawful transaction verification, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations. We may delete or minimize eligible records as appropriate and explain any limits that apply. Information held independently by third parties may require use of their own privacy request channels.

Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can investigate and respond appropriately. Advertising and data processing for minors depend on applicable law, age-related platform requirements, and the audience settings selected in Google Play and advertising services. A general-audience description does not override protections required for children.

The exact controls available may depend on the installed game version and region. Essential purchase verification and security processing do not depend on acceptance of optional gameplay analytics.

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal data; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a relevant data protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. Withdrawing consent does not automatically remove all previously processed provider data or necessary lawful transaction records.
