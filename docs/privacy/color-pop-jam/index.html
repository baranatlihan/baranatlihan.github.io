Privacy Policy – Color Pop Jam

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Color Pop Jam (Android package: com.baranatlihan.colorpopjam) is developed and published by Baran Atlıhan, an independent developer responsible for the game's data processing.

This policy explains how information is processed to operate the game, validate purchases, prevent duplicate grants and fraud, deliver advertising when enabled, and support privacy choices. It also describes optional analytics and diagnostics that may be enabled in future versions. Processing depends on the version of the game, enabled features, your region, and your choices. Some testing versions disable purchases, advertising, or optional services. GameAnalytics and Bugsnag are not collecting optional telemetry in the current game configuration.

Information We May Process

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, installation identifiers, IP address, and technical request or error information needed by enabled services. Services including Unity IAP and advertising providers may also derive an approximate country or location from network information.
  • Guest identifiers: a Firebase Authentication anonymous user ID and authentication information used to identify a guest securely when communicating with purchase services. An anonymous ID is a technical identifier, not a public profile or a guarantee that data cannot be linked to an installation.
  • Purchase information: product and transaction or order identifiers, purchase history, purchase tokens and store receipts (which may contain original transaction JSON and digital signatures), verification results, fulfillment, duplicate checks, and refund status. Records may be associated with a guest ID and installation identifier. The Unity IAP SDK may also process purchase-event information separately from our Firebase verification service.
  • Local game data: progress, coin balance, settings, and purchase fulfillment or entitlement state stored on your device. Local game progress is separate from the server's purchase ledger; that ledger is not a cloud backup of your game.
  • Advertising information, when ads are enabled: advertising identifiers where permitted, ad requests, impressions, interactions, and consent choices.
  • Optional gameplay analytics, if activated later: level starts, completions, failures, retries and revives; level difficulty and attempt counts; tutorial steps; coin and power-up stock gains, spending and use; advertising requests, displays, cancellations and rewarded outcomes; and verified purchase-related event categories. GameAnalytics may also process an SDK-generated pseudonymous identifier, session, platform, app and technical network data. Raw receipts, purchase tokens, payment card details and authentication secrets are not intended to be sent as custom gameplay analytics fields.
  • Optional crash diagnostics, if activated later: managed errors, native crashes and app-hang/ANR information supported by the platform; error category and stack trace; app version/build, Unity/runtime/OS and device characteristics; and limited gameplay context. Native SDKs and transport may handle technical identifiers and IP addresses. Application-side filtering limits sensitive fields, but does not guarantee all native SDK metadata and transport information are free of personal data.
  • Support information: your email address and any details you choose to send when contacting the developer.

Anonymous Guest Account and Recovery Limits

The game uses Firebase Authentication in Anonymous Guest mode for features such as authenticated purchase verification. You do not enter an email address or password to create this guest identity. The initial release does not offer Google sign-in, email sign-in, or account linking.

If you uninstall the game, clear its app data, or change devices, restoration of your progress and purchased coin balance is not guaranteed. These actions can remove local data or leave you with a new anonymous account. A Google Play purchase history does not by itself provide a backup of spent or remaining coins or gameplay progress.

Remove Ads is a permanent, non-consumable store product, but automatic transfer of ownership to a new anonymous account is not guaranteed. Any ownership check depends on available store evidence and the game's verification process. There is no guaranteed cross-device or reinstall recovery service in the initial release.

These technical recovery limitations do not limit your applicable consumer, refund, or data protection rights. Contact support for purchase problems; support cannot promise restoration that the available records do not support.

Google Play Purchases and Verification

Google Play handles payments for coin packs of 250, 750, and 2,000 coins and for Remove Ads, when purchases are enabled. Payment card details are handled by Google Play and its payment providers; the game does not directly collect your payment card details.

Purchase information is sent to Firebase Cloud Functions for server-side verification with Google Play. Cloud Firestore stores a transaction ledger to track verification and fulfillment, prevent duplicate grants, and handle refunded or revoked transactions. The service associates relevant records with the guest and installation involved in the purchase. Purchase verification and fraud prevention are separate from optional gameplay analytics. The Firestore purchase ledger records pseudonymous guest and transaction references, status and hashed identifiers; it is not intended to store raw purchase receipts or purchase tokens.

Unity In-App Purchasing Data Processing

When in-app purchases are enabled, Color Pop Jam also uses the Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP) SDK, version 5.4 or later. In addition to communicating with the platform store and our Firebase purchase-verification service, Unity IAP can transmit commerce and technical information to Unity for purchase processing, transaction reporting, service reliability, security, and diagnostics.

This information can include purchase history and events; product and transaction identifiers; original store receipt information, signed receipt data or purchase tokens where included in transaction payloads; Unity installation, session, player or device identifiers; device and app details; a country inferred from network information; and relevant crash, diagnostic, or performance information. Unity's handling of these data is separate from our optional gameplay analytics, even when those optional analytics are disabled. This does not mean that Unity receives payment card numbers from the game.

For the Color Pop Jam projects in Unity Dashboard, we have selected essential Developer Data collection and core product/service usage, disabled additional recommended data collection and optional broader usage, and have not granted additional Developer Data access to Unity Ads. These choices do not eliminate the essential processing required by Unity IAP. Depending on the processing activity, Unity may act as a service provider processing data on our behalf or as an independent data controller under its applicable terms and notices. Read Unity IAP's privacy explanation, Unity's privacy policy, and Unity IAP Additional Terms for more information.

Advertising and Consent

In the current Android Development integration, Google AdMob Direct is the selected advertising provider for rewarded and interstitial ads. Unity LevelPlay libraries may be included in the project but are not selected for mediation in that configuration. When advertising is enabled, AdMob and the advertising partners serving an ad may process device and network information, advertising identifiers where permitted, and ad interaction information to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Future versions may use different providers, and the applicable disclosures will be updated.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable advertising consent messages and privacy options. Advertising that requires consent depends on the applicable consent state. The availability of personalized, limited, or other advertising depends on the region, enabled providers, and privacy choices; declining personalization does not necessarily remove all ads or all technical processing.

Where available or required, use the game's Privacy Options control to review or change advertising consent. You can also manage advertising ID and ad privacy settings on your Android device. Buying Remove Ads is an advertising entitlement, not a request to delete data or change consent.

Optional Gameplay Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

Current status: optional GameAnalytics and Bugsnag collection is disabled. The game includes preparation for GameAnalytics gameplay analytics and Bugsnag error monitoring. However, independent privacy reviews, player-choice controls, provider configuration and collection switches have not all been activated in the current configuration. No optional analytics or crash reports are intended to be sent to these services by that configuration. AppsFlyer attribution, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are likewise not active optional services. Technical processing by necessary purchases, security and enabled advertising services remains separate.

GameAnalytics, if enabled in a future version: We may measure level start/completion/failure, retry and revive outcomes, difficulty and attempt counts, tutorial steps, coin and power-up activity, advertising lifecycle and reward fulfillment, and limited verified purchase event categories. Purposes include improving level difficulty, onboarding, in-game economy balance and reliability. Development test purchases are not treated as real production revenue. Custom events are designed to avoid raw receipts, purchase tokens, authentication secrets and contact details. Nevertheless, the SDK and network service may process pseudonymous user/installation identifiers, session information, device/app details and technical network information. Disabling advertising ID tracking does not necessarily eliminate other identifiers. See GameAnalytics developer privacy information.

Bugsnag, if enabled in a future version: We may process handled and unhandled errors, supported native crash and app-hang/ANR reports, error categories, stack traces, app version/build, device/OS/runtime characteristics, and limited game-state context to diagnose and fix stability problems. We plan to limit automatic user identity, session tracking and unnecessary breadcrumbs and to redact sensitive fields in reports. However, the native platform integrations and network service may still process generated identifiers, app/device metadata and IP-related network information. See Bugsnag's Unity data documentation and SmartBear's privacy information.

Independent privacy choices: Google UMP advertising consent does not authorize optional gameplay analytics or crash diagnostics. Before enabling optional collection in a future version, we will review the provider arrangements and applicable legal basis, update required notices, and offer separate analytics and crash diagnostic choices where applicable. Choosing one will not automatically choose the other. The current game configuration does not yet provide these optional telemetry switches because collection is off. Essential purchase and security features will not require optional analytics consent.

Withdrawal and native crash limits: A future Bugsnag-enabled integration may have in-flight network requests or native crash reports saved before a choice is withdrawn. Closing future capture/submission cannot guarantee immediate cancellation or deletion of those prior records. We will evaluate native offline caching, restart behavior, retention and deletion before enabling crash collection. An in-game preference will not be described as instant erasure of records already generated or received.

Other operational processing: Enabled online services may still process technical request, error, and security logs for authentication, purchase verification, advertising, or support. This is distinct from optional game-managed telemetry. Firebase Remote Config may retrieve game settings and process installation/request data needed for that service; it does not provide account linking or a progress backup.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until removed by game or device actions. Uninstalling the game or clearing app data can remove local records, but does not automatically delete server-side purchase records or information already held by Google Play or other service providers.

Guest and transaction records are retained as needed to operate purchase verification, prevent duplicate fulfillment and fraud, handle refunds or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the record and its purpose; there is currently no automatic expiration or one-click deletion process for the purchase ledger. This policy does not promise an automatic deletion deadline or permanent storage. We review verified requests and delete or minimize data where appropriate, while retaining records where needed for lawful purchase verification, refunds, fraud prevention, duplicate-grant protection, or legal obligations. Service providers also apply their own retention practices.

If optional gameplay analytics or crash reporting is enabled in a later version, the relevant provider may retain event data, technical identifiers, and temporarily queued or cached records under its service arrangements and retention settings. Before activation, we will review and disclose the applicable retention and deletion process. Turning off future collection does not automatically erase records already lawfully received by a provider; you may contact us about applicable data access or deletion rights.

To request access to or deletion of data associated with your guest identity, email baranatlihan9@gmail.com and name Color Pop Jam. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify that you are entitled to make the request and locate the relevant anonymous account or purchase records. If you have already lost access to your guest identity, identifying the relevant records may be difficult. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, full purchase tokens, or payment card details.

Requests are handled subject to applicable law. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes; we will explain any applicable limits. You may also need to manage information held independently by Google Play or another provider through that provider's privacy controls.

Third-Party Service Providers

Information is processed by providers needed for enabled features, including:

  • Google and Google Play: payment processing, store purchase records, and advertising or consent services such as AdMob and UMP.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: anonymous authentication, Cloud Functions purchase verification, Firestore transaction records, Remote Config, and related operational processing.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing: purchase-event and related technical data processing, service delivery and transaction reporting when purchases are enabled. Unity may act as a processor or an independent controller depending on the activity.
  • Google AdMob and participating advertising providers: ad delivery, measurement and consent processing when advertising is enabled. Unity LevelPlay may be installed but is not selected in the current Android Development integration.
  • GameAnalytics: optional gameplay progression, economy and advertising analytics only if independently enabled in a future version.
  • Bugsnag / SmartBear: optional error and crash diagnostics only if independently enabled in a future version.

GameAnalytics and Bugsnag are described as conditional future providers, not active collectors in the current configuration. AppsFlyer, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are likewise not active optional services. Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies and may process it outside your country. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims.

Children's Privacy

Color Pop Jam is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal contact information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps. Applicable age, regional, and store requirements govern advertising and consent; a general-audience designation does not authorize unrestricted collection from children.

Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You can review available in-game advertising privacy options, change Android advertising privacy settings, manage purchases through Google Play, and contact us about your data. Optional GameAnalytics and Bugsnag controls are not yet offered in the current configuration because those providers are disabled; separate choices will be required before a future collecting release where applicable. Withdrawing consent affects subsequent processing based on that consent; it does not automatically remove information lawfully processed earlier or every native SDK record already queued. Essential purchase and security processing does not depend on opting into optional analytics or crash diagnostics.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact the developer to exercise applicable rights. You do not need to waive these rights to use support or request help with a purchase.
