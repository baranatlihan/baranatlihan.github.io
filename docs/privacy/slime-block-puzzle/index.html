Privacy Policy – Slime Block Puzzle

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Slime Block Puzzle is developed and published by Baran Atlıhan, an independent developer.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed when you use the game, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We May Process

Depending on your device, region, privacy choices, and the features you use, the game and its service providers may process information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Game usage and interaction events
  • Level progress and gameplay events
  • Crash reports, diagnostics, and performance information
  • Advertising impressions and interactions
  • Purchase and transaction status
  • Anonymous technical account identifiers

We do not require players to create a visible account to play the game.

Advertising

The game may display advertisements using mediation and advertising services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity LevelPlay
  • Unity Ads
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Mintegral

Advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interaction data, and similar information as permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices.

Where required, the game asks for consent before requesting advertising that requires consent.

Analytics and Attribution

We may use analytics and attribution services to understand how the game is used, improve gameplay, measure technical performance, and understand advertising effectiveness.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • GameAnalytics
  • AppsFlyer

Analytics or attribution collection that requires consent is enabled only according to the applicable privacy settings and user choices.

Crash Reporting and Remote Configuration

We may use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and technical problems.

Firebase Remote Config may be used to remotely configure non-sensitive game settings and feature behavior.

Anonymous Firebase Account

The game may create a silent anonymous Firebase Authentication account for technical purposes such as secure purchase processing and recovery.

This does not create a public profile and does not require the player to enter an email address, password, or other visible login information.

An anonymous account may not provide permanent cross-device or uninstall recovery unless it is later linked to a supported permanent account.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by the platform store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

The game may process transaction identifiers and purchase status information required to validate purchases and deliver purchased content.

Payment card information is handled by the platform store and is not directly collected by the game.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your region, you may be presented with privacy and consent options before certain advertising or analytics services are activated.

Where required, the game provides a way to reopen privacy options and change your choices.

On iOS, the game may also request App Tracking Transparency permission before accessing information that Apple defines as tracking-related.

Children's Privacy

The game is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal contact information directly to us.

Advertising and data processing may be adjusted according to applicable age, store, and regional requirements.
